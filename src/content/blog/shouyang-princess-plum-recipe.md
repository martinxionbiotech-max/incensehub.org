## The Formula, Deconstructed

**Direct answer:** The 香乘 records 甘松半两、白芷半两、牡丹皮半两、藁本半两、茴香一两、丁皮一两、檀香一两、降真香二钱、白梅一百枚 — four herbal middles at half a liǎng, a spice-and-wood backbone at a full liǎng, a resin accent at two qián, and the plum counted in pieces rather than weight.

The method is simple to state: except for the clove bark, roast everything dry, grind to a coarse powder, and age (窨) in a sealed vessel for a month before burning.

| Role | Ingredient | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 君 (chief) | 檀香 sandalwood | Supplies the warm woody body |
| 臣 (minister) | 甘松 / 白芷 / 藁本 spikenard, angelica, lovage | Adds herbal, slightly medicinal middle tones |
| 佐 (assistant) | 白梅 + 牡丹皮 white plum, peony bark | Gives the tart-sweet "plum" character — the accord's signature |
| 使 (guide) | 茴香 / 丁皮 / 降真香 fennel, clove bark, rosewood | Adds spice and fixative notes that bind and lift |

Two mechanism chains explain how the "plum" is assembled.

**Chain one — why the flower is replaced by the fruit.** Plum blossoms are too delicate to carry a scent through burning; their fragrance is faint even fresh and collapses under heat. The recipe reaches instead for 白梅, a pickled or dried white plum whose tart-sweet, slightly salty fruit character *does* survive combustion → that stable fruit note becomes the recognizable "plum" signal → layered over sandalwood's warm body it reads as plum rather than as pickled fruit. The fruit stands in for the flower because it is the version of "plum" that can actually be burned.

**Chain two — the fresh-over-warm contrast.** Sandalwood gives the blend a warm, woody floor; the herbal middles (spikenard, angelica, lovage) supply a faintly medicinal green layer. Onto that, the white plum and peony bark introduce a tart, clean, almost cool note → the cool-tart accent against the warm-woody base is what the nose recognizes as "plum blossom" → the fennel and clove bark at the guide position tie the two registers together and add lift. The plum character is a contrast effect, built by placing a tart fruit note over a warm wood.
